QuickQR Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-03-21

Core principle: QuickQR primarily processes scan data on-device. Scan history is stored locally on your device.

1. Information We Process

To provide scanning and history features, the app stores the following data locally:

  • Scanned content (text, URL, vCard, etc.)
  • Scan timestamp and detected type

We do not intentionally collect direct personal identity data such as your name, phone number, or ID number.

2. Permissions

  • Camera: used to scan QR/bar codes.
  • Contacts (optional): used only when you choose to save scanned vCard contacts.

You can revoke these permissions at any time in system settings.

3. Ads and Third-Party Services

The Android version may show Google ads (for example, startup ads and banner ads). Related identifiers may be processed according to Google policies. The iOS version currently does not show ads.

For details, please refer to Google AdMob privacy documentation.

4. Data Storage and Security

Scan history is stored locally on your device. We do not upload your history to our own server.

Please keep your device secure to prevent unauthorized access.

5. Your Rights

  • You can clear scan history in-app.
  • You can uninstall the app to remove local data.

6. Policy Updates

We may update this policy from time to time and show the latest version in-app.
