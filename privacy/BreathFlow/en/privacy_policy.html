Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 7, 2026

Introduction

Welcome to BreathFlow (hereinafter referred to as "we" or "this app"). We deeply value your personal privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information.

Important Notice: All data in this app is stored locally on your device and will not be uploaded to any server. We do not collect, transmit, or share any of your personal data.

Information Collection

To provide a complete functional experience, this app stores the following information locally on your device:

Training Records

Includes training date, duration, and breathing scheme name used. Used to display your training statistics and consecutive training days.

App Settings

Includes sound prompts, vibration feedback, voice guidance preferences, and daily training goals and reminder times.

Usage Preferences

Includes your favorite breathing schemes and last used scheme, to provide you with a more convenient experience.

We do NOT collect:

  • Name, email, phone number, or other personal identification information
  • Device identifiers (IDFA, IDFV, etc.)
  • Geographic location information
  • Browsing history or usage analytics data
  • Any form of sensitive personal information

Device Permissions

This app requires the following device permissions to provide full functionality:

Permission Purpose Required
Microphone Captures ambient noise to help you understand if the current environment is suitable for breathing exercises No (can be disabled in settings)
Calendar Creates daily training reminder events to help you develop a regular breathing exercise habit No (can be disabled in settings)

You can revoke these permissions at any time in system settings. After revoking permissions, related features will not function properly, but other app features will not be affected.

Data Storage and Security

We take the following measures to protect your data security:

  • Local Storage: All data is stored locally on your device using secure storage mechanisms provided by the system (SharedPreferences/iOS Keychain)
  • No Network Transmission: This app does not have network data transmission capabilities; your data never leaves your device
  • No Third-Party SDKs: We do not integrate any third-party analytics or advertising SDKs
  • No User Tracking: We do not use any form of user tracking technology

Data Control

You have complete control over your data:

  • View Data: You can view all training records and statistics within the app
  • Delete Data: You can delete all local data by uninstalling the app
  • Export Data: Data export functionality is currently not supported

Children's Privacy

This app is not designed for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since this app does not collect any personal identification information, there are no special handling requirements for children's personal information protection. If you are a parent or guardian and have any concerns about your child using this app, please feel free to uninstall it.

Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated policy will be published within the app. If you continue to use this app, you agree to accept the revised policy. We recommend that you review this policy regularly to stay informed of the latest information.
